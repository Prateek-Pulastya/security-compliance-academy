## Overview

A **trust boundary** is any point where data or requests move between zones of differing trust — the
internet and your VPC, one service and another, user space and the kernel. Threats concentrate at
these crossings because that's where assumptions can be violated.

## Common boundaries

- Client ↔ server (the browser is untrusted)
- Your app ↔ a third-party API or [MCP server](/ai-security/mcp-security)
- Between microservices or tenants
- Application ↔ database
- One cloud account/VPC ↔ another

## What to do at each crossing

- **Authenticate** the other side and **authorize** the request (least privilege).
- **Validate and encode** everything crossing the boundary — treat it as untrusted.
- **Encrypt** data in transit.
- **Log** the crossing for audit.

Draw boundaries explicitly on your [data-flow diagram](/threat-modeling/data-flow-diagrams), then
apply [STRIDE](/threat-modeling/stride) at each one.
