## Overview

**ISO/IEC 42001** is the first international **AI management system (AIMS)** standard. It applies the
familiar ISO management-system structure (as in ISO 27001) to the responsible development and use of
AI, and it is certifiable.

## What it adds

Beyond security, it requires organizations to govern AI systematically across the lifecycle:
AI policy, roles, **AI impact assessment**, data governance, and continual improvement.

## Where it fits

- [NIST AI RMF](/ai-security/nist-ai-rmf) — the risk-management functions you operate.
- **ISO/IEC 42001** — the certifiable management system that institutionalizes them.
- [EU AI Act](/ai-security/eu-ai-act) — the legal obligations in the EU.
