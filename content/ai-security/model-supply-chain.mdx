## Overview

An AI system inherits risk from everything it is built on: base models, fine-tuning data, embeddings,
libraries, and plugins. OWASP lists **Supply Chain** as a top LLM risk because a compromise upstream
becomes a compromise in your app.

## Where risk enters

- **Pretrained models** — a model from an untrusted source may be backdoored or poisoned.
- **Datasets** — poisoned training/fine-tuning data biases or backdoors behaviour.
- **Third-party plugins / tools** — extend capability and attack surface at once.
- **Model artifacts** — unsafe serialization formats can execute code on load.

## Defenses

- **Provenance** — source models and datasets from trusted, verifiable origins.
- **Integrity** — verify checksums/signatures; prefer safe artifact formats.
- **Inventory** — maintain an AI bill of materials (models, data, dependencies).
- **Isolation** — load and evaluate untrusted models in a sandbox.

This mirrors classic software supply-chain security — see
[Secure SDLC](/secure-sdlc) — applied to models and data.
