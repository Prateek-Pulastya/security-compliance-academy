## Overview

Detection and response depend on telemetry. In the cloud, an **API audit trail** plus workload and
network logs give you the visibility to spot compromise and prove compliance.

## Core practices

- **Turn on the API audit trail** in every account/region and protect it from tampering.
- **Centralize logs** into one account/workspace with restricted access and retention.
- **Enable managed threat detection** for anomaly and known-bad detection.
- **Alert, don't just collect** — route high-signal events to on-call.
