## Overview

**ISO/IEC 42001** is the first international **AI management system (AIMS)** standard. It follows the
same structure as ISO 27001, applied to the responsible development and use of AI.

## Purpose

Help organizations govern AI systematically — managing risks and opportunities across the AI
lifecycle while addressing trustworthiness concerns such as fairness, transparency, and safety.

## Architecture

Like ISO 27001, it pairs **management-system clauses** with an **Annex** of AI-specific controls and
implementation guidance, plus references to AI impact assessment.

## Core Concepts

- **AI management system (AIMS)** — the governance structure for AI.
- **AI impact assessment** — evaluating effects on individuals, groups, and society.

## Cloud Mapping

Controls for data governance, logging, and access apply to managed AI/ML platforms (Amazon
SageMaker, Azure ML, Google Vertex AI) and to model and dataset access controls.
