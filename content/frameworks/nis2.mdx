## Overview

**NIS2** is the EU directive that expands and strengthens the original NIS Directive, raising the
cybersecurity baseline for **essential** and **important** entities across more sectors.

## Purpose

Achieve a high common level of cybersecurity across the EU by mandating risk-management measures,
incident reporting, and stronger supervision and enforcement.

## Architecture

Core obligations include **risk-management measures** (Article 21), **incident reporting** with
staged deadlines (Article 23), and **governance** accountability for management bodies.

## Core Concepts

- **Essential vs. important entities** — determines the supervisory regime and penalties.
- **Supply-chain security** — measures must address risks from suppliers and service providers.

## Cloud Mapping

Article 21 measures (access control, encryption, MFA, logging) map to native controls in AWS,
Azure, GCP, and Kubernetes; incident-reporting timelines drive detection and alerting design.
